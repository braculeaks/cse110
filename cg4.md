## how to get cg 4.0 ?

1. Don't miss Classes.
    1. if you can join extra classes in other sections.
        - Teachers in BRAC (specially in the CSE department) are really nice
        - They won't ever be bothered if you join their classes. Rather,
        - Sometimes you'd be really surprised how happy they'd be to be able to teach you
            - (because they can simply see the effort that you are putting, and they embrace that so much)

    2. in case you miss any class 
        - watch previous lectures on youtube.
        - just search up `cse110 BRAC` on yt. you will see a heck ton of stuff

    3. if anything feels hard, check the very last point of this numbered list 
 
2. Solve lab assignments on your own.
    - Don't use AI
    - Don't ask friends at the first glance.
    - Try die hard for at least 15-30minutes per problem.
        - If you can come up with some code/psuedocode --> then, that's really nice.
        - then, even if you can't, now you can ask friends for help :)
        - ..

3. Solve `2.pset - ST_Badhon` ➖ Source: [🔗](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tdEqocBsaDPSstySABCduXZ0DxeHKz1k)

4. Solve Past Quiz & LabExam Questions ➖ `3.2.exams.labs` & `3.2.quiz`
    - You won't ever have to solve "All of them".
    - BRAC is simply not like that.
    - Before Every TheoryQuiz / LabQuiz
        - just solve 3/4 quiz as sample/practice
    - Before mid/Final
        - focus on Practise Sheets
        - solve 3/4 quiz as sample/practice
    - Just practising is enough. 
    - If you can do the Lab Assignments on your own, without any AI / friends explanations.
        - Then you probably won't even need to solve past Quiz/LabExams

5. Solve `2.pSet` (which is central)
    1. you can look into `1.2.notes` if you want. Solve extra problems from there too. but not "required".
    2. most of the pSet here are garbage.... 
    3. ST_Badhon psets are the main pSets.

6. Solve `3.exams`
